Plan Calendar

This is an offline preview. The Ask bar and the Preview Calendar are not built yet, so there is nothing to type or click on this page today.

How it will work

  1. You will tell the Ask bar what you need, for example "find time to work out for half an hour".
  2. Your plan will appear in the Preview Calendar next to your existing events. You will be able to drag a step or ask for a change.
  3. Nothing will be added to Google Calendar until you confirm.

All times are shown in UTC.
